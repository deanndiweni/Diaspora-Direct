Delete your Diaspora Direct account

Delete it in the app

Sign in, open Profile, select Delete account, and confirm. This permanently closes your account.

Request deletion without the app

Email us from the address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

Your authentication account, profile, messages, and service requests are deleted. If you are an agent, your agent profile is also deleted and open work is returned to the queue.

Information we may retain

We may retain limited transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal records where required by law. Any retained information is restricted to those purposes and removed when the applicable retention period ends.
