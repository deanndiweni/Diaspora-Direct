## Shared description draft (use for both stores' long description field)

Diaspora Direct connects people abroad with a vetted local agent on the ground in Zimbabwe, so you can look after family, property and errands back home without waiting on a favour or hoping a phone call gets answered.

Book directly in the app - an agent typically confirms within 2 hours. Pay securely in the app.

What you can book:
- Property checks - a trusted visit to confirm a house, plot or building is secure and well kept
- Family welfare - a home visit to check on a relative, with an honest report back
- Errands - collecting documents, running local tasks, and handling day-to-day jobs you can't do from abroad
- Urgent support - fast help when something can't wait
- Documents - collecting, delivering or processing paperwork on your behalf
- Funeral care - dignified, practical support arranging and attending funeral matters

In-house specialists, on call:
- In-house doctor - emergency callouts and home visits across Zimbabwe
- In-house lawyer - legal cover for arrests, bail, court and defence matters

Every agent is vetted before they can take on work, and once you're matched you can message your agent directly so you always know what's happening. Pricing is shown before you book, with no surprises.

Diaspora Direct is built and operated by Diaspora Direct Ltd, a UK company, for the diaspora community supporting family and property in Zimbabwe.
